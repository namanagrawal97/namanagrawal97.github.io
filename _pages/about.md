I am a 6th Year PhD candidate in Department of Neurobiology and Behavior. I am passionate about teaching Neurobiology through experiential hands-on learning and revision-based learning. I have been an Instructor of Record for 3 Semesters - designing, teaching, and refining my firstyear writing seminar course - _Big Brain Moments_ - teaching a **socio-political history of neuroscience**. I have also been a Head TA for _Introduction to Behavior_ and a TA for _Introduction to Neuroscience_. 
My research interests include computational analysis of neural signals during complex behaviors. I am interested in understanding how brain areas exchange information, and how we can correlate this information exchange with behavior.
